
# FactoryPulse Manufacturing Intelligence Platform

## 1. Project Overview
FactoryPulse is a Business Intelligence and Predictive Analytics platform designed to help manufacturing companies improve operational performance through data-driven decisions.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Monitor production performance.
- Reduce machine downtime.
- Improve inventory management.
- Monitor product quality.
- Analyze sales and customer demand.
- Support management decisions.

## 3. Business Environment
- Factories: 3
- Production lines: 8
- Machines: 30
- Products: 50
- Suppliers: 20

## 4. Business Processes
1. Production
2. Inventory
3. Maintenance
4. Quality
5. Sales and Orders

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Production Achievement
- Defect Rate
- Machine Downtime
- Inventory Value
- Revenue
- Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
